Chocolate Cake

Prep Time: 45 min | Serves: 6

⏳ Time Left: 45:00

    Ingredients 🍫

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk

1. Mix dry ingredients.

2. Add eggs and milk.

3. Pour into pan and bake at 180°C.

4. Cool & serve with frosting.
